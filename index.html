Our Story

A tiny room with a big appetite

Howdys opened in June 2026 in the former Fabrik space on East MLK. Chef Emily Rayburn runs a counter-service, no-reservations room of snug two-seat tables — 25 to 35 seats — where the menu turns over every month or two with the Central Texas seasons.

  • ✓

    Chef Emily Rayburn — Italian-inspired cooking spotlighting Central Texas produce and purveyors (official site).

  • ✓

    Always changing — The menu is supposed to change every few months — new pies and pastas rotate in (The Infatuation).

  • ✓

    House-made GF dough — The gluten-free pizza crust is made in house (Restaurantji guest reviews).
